To build a strong foundation in modern technology, [Samsung Innovation Campus](https://samsungsic-thailand.org/) empowers educators and learners with essential future-ready digital skills. We drive an in-school project that equips school teachers with active learning workshops, modernized teaching materials, funding, and mentorship to implement core ICT subjects, including Computational Thinking, Media Literacy, and Cybersecurity, directly in the classroom.
