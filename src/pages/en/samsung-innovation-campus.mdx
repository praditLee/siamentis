[Samsung Innovation Campus](https://samsungsic-thailand.org/) is a transformative ICT education program that equips educators and learners with future-ready digital skills, preparing them to successfully navigate a rapidly-changing digital landscape. The program was designed to feature both an in-school classroom implementation component as well as an online, free-to-access platform to supplement learning.

The in-school component of the program transforms the ICT classroom from the typical passive, lecture-based format into one where students are active participants in group work and discussions surrounding important ICT topics such as computational thinking, media literacy, and cybersecurity. The in-school program provides hands-on training to ICT teachers across Thailand and provides support as they implement a modernized ICT curriculum in their own classrooms using the active learning framework. The program emphasizes relatability in its curriculum, and puts a focus on making sure students see how different ICT topics can have an effect on their own lives, both digital and offline.
